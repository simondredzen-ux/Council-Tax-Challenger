4. WHY "HOUSING" (and what it costs us)
Meta requires a Special Ad Category for housing-related ads shown in the UK, and that category covers property
appraisal/valuation-type services. Our ads are about property valuations, so declaring it is the safe choice.
Getting the category wrong is a common cause of rejection or account restriction.
Effect: fixed age 18-65+, all genders, no postcode targeting, limited detailed targeting, no lookalike audiences.
We can't target high-value postcodes on Meta at all; the copy has to attract the right owners.
Uncertain: the UK minimum radius (10 vs 15 miles) is disputed in third-party guides, but it doesn't matter
when targeting a whole region (England).
