## Draft ads (responsive search ads; all within Google's limits)

**Message A: the April 2026 date and preparing evidence (recommended first).**

Headlines:
1. Mansion Tax: April 2026 Values
2. Check Your Likely Band Free
3. Prepare Your Evidence Now
4. Free Mansion Tax Checker
5. Bands Use April 2026 Values
6. Charge Due From April 2028
7. From £2,500 a Year
8. Prime London Property Expert
9. England Homes Worth £2m+
10. Free Personal Report

Descriptions:
1. Bands are due to use April 2026 values. Check your likely band and yearly charge free.
2. Draft bands are expected in autumn 2027. Keep evidence of your home's 2026 value now.
3. Free checker and report. Optional £199 Evidence Pack, personally reviewed. No success fee.
4. Independent: not connected with HMRC or the Valuation Office. Takes about a minute.

**Message B: being near a threshold.**

Headlines:
1. Near a Mansion Tax Threshold?
2. Just Over £2m? Check Your Band
3. Check Your Likely Band Free
4. No Sliding Scale Between Bands
5. Free Mansion Tax Checker
6. Thresholds: £2m to £5m+
7. From £2,500 a Year
8. Prime London Property Expert

Descriptions:
1. A home just over a threshold pays the whole higher charge. Check your likely band free.
2. Estimate your home's April 2026 value from nearby completed sales, then get your report.
3. Free checker and report. Optional £199 Evidence Pack, personally reviewed. No success fee.
4. Charges £2,500 to £7,500 a year from April 2028, under the government's plans.

**Checked against the fact-check of 16 Nov:**
- April 2026 values; start in April 2028; draft bands expected in autumn 2027;
- bands starting at £2m;
- charges of £2,500 to £7,500.

No ad says values have been issued or that appeals are open. ChatGPT's headline "Could Your Home Be Wrongly Valued for the 2028 Mansion Tax?" is not used: at 58 characters it is too long for a headline (limit 30), and "wrongly valued" implies official values already exist.

**Why A first:** most searches are about the threshold and the cost (group B), and A gives a reason to act now (the April 2026 evidence). B suits the threshold searches; test it second, or run both with even rotation once there is volume.
